Provider marks in the model picker and Settings > Connections

The app bundles small provider marks (ios/App/Assets.xcassets/ProviderMarks)
only to identify the provider named beside each one. No endorsement is
implied, and each brand keeps its trademark rights. Atum's eclipse is not part
of this set. The files are the ones the Atum desktop picker bundles
(atum-platform/atum-desktop apps/desktop/src/matrix/provider-marks at
2365efe8), byte-identical except where noted.

Simple Icons (CC0-1.0), simple-icons/simple-icons commit
90646e4bf7f3b89df6c37e65baf3e7bc759a517c: claude.svg, deepseek.svg,
gemini.svg (icons/googlegemini.svg), minimax.svg (custom
permission recorded at simple-icons/simple-icons#13982), opencode.svg,
qwen.svg. CC0 does not waive the brands' trademark rights. Licence text:
ProviderMarks-SimpleIcons-CC0-1.0.txt.

Lobe Icons (MIT), lobehub/lobe-icons commit
8b7bbc433d38437fa2b0cca58ae896917162b812: openai.svg, grok.svg
(packages/static-svg/icons). Modification: fill="currentColor" is written as
fill="#000000" so the iOS asset compiler keeps the shape; the app draws both as
template images in the theme's ink. OpenAI's mark remains subject to
https://openai.com/brand.
